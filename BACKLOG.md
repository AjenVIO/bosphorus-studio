## Rules for adding topics
- Prefer topics with public-domain paintings, miniatures, maps or pre-1930 photographs.
- One clear question per episode ("Why…?", "How…?", "What happened when…?").
- Avoid living people, modern politics and anything only sourced to one popular website.
